Software Engineering Intern – Platform & Reliability (m/f/d)
Nimbus Logistics · Berlin, Germany · Internship · 5 months · Start: March 2027 · Hybrid

About Nimbus Logistics
Nimbus Logistics builds a route-optimisation platform used by more than 200 retailers across Europe. Our services compute millions of delivery routes every day and have to stay fast and reliable.

What you will do
You will join the Platform team and work on:
- Building internal tooling in Python and Go to automate deployments to Kubernetes
- Extending our monitoring stack (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry) and writing alerts and runbooks
- Improving our CI/CD pipelines on GitHub Actions
- Taking part in the on-call rotation as an observer and in post-incident reviews

What we are looking for
- Final-year student in computer science or a related field
- Good knowledge of Linux, Docker and one scripting language
- Experience with Terraform or any cloud provider (AWS) is a plus
- Fluent English; German is a nice-to-have

Practical information
Duration: 4 to 6 months, starting between February and March 2027. Paid internship.
